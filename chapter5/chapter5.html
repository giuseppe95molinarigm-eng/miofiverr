Week 1 – Gentle Beginnings

Goal of the weekLearn the foundations of Tai Chi—posture, breathing, and a few simple movements. By the end of this week, you will have your very first 10-minute routine.
Day1

Breathing and Posture Basics

PurposeBuild awareness of posture and learn how to coordinate breath with gentle arm movements.

Standing Version

  1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart.
  2. Keep knees slightly bent, arms resting naturally at your sides.
  3. Inhale gently through the nose as you raise both arms in front of you, palms facing down, until they reach shoulder height.
  4. Exhale softly through the mouth as you lower the arms back to your sides.
  5. Repeat 5–10 times, moving as slowly as your breath.

Seated Version

  1. Sit upright on a sturdy chair, feet flat on the floor.
  2. Inhale as you raise your arms to shoulder height.
  3. Exhale as you lower them back down.
  4. Repeat 5–10 times.
Practice Notes

This may feel very simple, but it is the foundation of Tai Chi. Focus on calm breathing and a relaxed posture. Imagine you are gently lifting and lowering your arms through warm water.

Day2

Wave Hands Like Clouds (Basic Form)

PurposeIntroduce gentle coordination of arms and body, while shifting weight naturally.

Standing Version

  1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, knees slightly bent.
  2. Shift weight gently to the left foot.
  3. Raise the left hand to shoulder height, palm facing inward.
  4. Slowly move the left hand across the body, as if brushing through a cloud.
  5. At the same time, the right hand lowers at your side, palm facing outward.
  6. Shift weight to the right foot and switch hands.
  7. Continue side to side for 6–8 cycles.

Seated Version

  1. Sit upright with feet flat.
  2. Raise the left hand to shoulder height, sweeping across your body.
  3. Lower it as the right hand rises.
  4. Continue side to side, keeping a gentle rhythm.
Practice Notes

Keep the movements soft and flowing. Imagine you are painting the air in front of you, each stroke smooth and graceful.

Day3

Shoulder and Arm Release

PurposeLoosen stiff shoulders, relieve tension, and improve circulation.

Standing Version

  1. Stand tall with feet apart.
  2. Roll both shoulders backward in slow circles, 6 times.
  3. Reverse direction, rolling forward 6 times.
  4. Raise arms to the side as you inhale, lower them as you exhale.

Seated Version

  1. Sit upright.
  2. Perform shoulder rolls forward and backward.
  3. Add gentle arm raises, in time with your breathing.
Practice Notes

Release any stiffness. Imagine tension melting from your shoulders with each roll.

Day4

Opening and Closing Hands

PurposeImprove coordination and relaxation through simple hand expansion and contraction.

Standing Version

  1. Stand with arms bent in front of the chest, palms facing each other.
  2. Inhale as you slowly open the hands outward, widening the space.
  3. Exhale as you bring the hands back together.
  4. Repeat 10 times.

Seated Version

  • Same as standing, performed while seated upright.
Practice Notes

Imagine holding a soft ball of energy between your palms, expanding and contracting with each breath.
