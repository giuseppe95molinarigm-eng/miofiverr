### Descrizione del progetto (da incollare)

> A niche cookbook interior for the trending "edible candle" market (butter, tallow and cocoa-butter candles you light at the table and then dip into), built to be uploaded straight to Amazon KDP.
>
> **Client goal:** a premium, giftable recipe book that looks like it belongs next to bestselling hardback cookbooks, while staying ink-efficient and fully KDP-compliant.
>
> **What I delivered:**
> • Recipe development and copywriting in a consistent voice (intro, timings, ingredients, method, chef's notes)
> • A reusable recipe template: serves / prep / set / burn-time bar, two-column ingredients and method
> • Custom vector illustrations for every recipe (sharp at any print size, no 300 DPI issues)
> • Front matter: title page, copyright with safety disclaimer, contents, introduction, tools & safety, base method, burn-time chart
> • Full-bleed chapter openers, feature pages and a measurement-conversion reference
> • Print-ready PDF: 8 × 10 in trim with 0.125 in bleed, mirrored gutter margins, embedded fonts
>
> **Challenge:** the topic involves an open flame, so safety had to be built into the design without scaring readers. I solved it with a friendly "five golden rules" page, burn times on every recipe and a clear disclaimer on the copyright page.
