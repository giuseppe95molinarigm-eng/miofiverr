Introduction

Welcome to the
Autumn Table

When the leaves turn copper and the evenings draw in, there is no better reason to gather friends around the table than a candle you can eat.

An edible candle is shaped from butter, tallow or cocoa butter and set around a food-safe cotton wick. Light it as your guests sit down, and within minutes a warm, fragrant pool forms at the top, ready for torn bread, roasted vegetables or crisp slices of apple. What starts as the centerpiece quietly becomes the first course.

I began making them for our family’s Thanksgiving table, and they have been the most talked-about dish every year since. Every recipe in this book has been tested in my own kitchen for flavor, for a clean and steady burn, and for how beautiful it looks in candlelight.

You will find burn times, make-ahead notes and seasonal pairings alongside each recipe. Start with the base method on page 8, then let the season guide you.

With warmth from my kitchen to yours,
Lauren

Savory Butter Candles

Herbed Garlic
Butter Crown
Candle

Shaped in a fluted mini bundt mold, this golden crown of garlic butter is flecked with thyme, rosemary and pink peppercorns. As it melts, finish the pool with a thread of warm honey and dip in thick slices of rustic bread.

Serves6–8
Prep20 min
Set4 hrs
Burn1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (225 g) salted butter, softened
  • 3 garlic cloves, finely grated
  • 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 2 tsp fresh rosemary, minced
  • 1 tsp pink peppercorns, crushed
  • ½ tsp flaky sea salt
  • To finish
  • 2 tbsp warm honey
  • Rustic bread, torn, for dipping
  • To assemble
  • 1 food-safe cotton wick, 6 in, oiled
  • 1 silicone mini bundt mold, 1 cup

Method

  1. 1

    Beat the softened butter until smooth and glossy, then fold in the garlic, herbs, peppercorns and salt.

  2. 2

    Thread the oiled wick through the center tube of the mold and secure it straight with a clothespin across the top.

  3. 3

    Pack the butter into the mold in three layers, pressing into every flute and tapping out air pockets.

  4. 4

    Chill for 4 hours, or freeze for 1 hour, until completely firm. Peel the mold away gently.

  5. 5

    Set the crown on a rimmed stoneware dish, trim the wick to ¼ in and light 10 minutes before serving. Drizzle the melted pool with honey.

Lauren’s tip Chill the mold for 10 minutes before filling: the flutes stay crisp and the crown releases cleanly every time.

Savory Butter Candles

Smoky Chili &
Mustard Seed
Candle

Golden with smoked paprika and studded with crunchy mustard seeds, this slow-burning tallow candle is crowned with chili flakes and toasted seeds. Pair it with roasted squash, pretzel bites and a glass of hard cider.

Serves6
Prep25 min
Set2 hrs
Burn90+ min

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup (150 g) rendered beef tallow
  • ¼ cup (55 g) unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ¼ tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tbsp whole yellow mustard seeds
  • ½ tsp smoked sea salt
  • Seed crown
  • 1 tsp red chili flakes
  • 1 tsp toasted pumpkin seeds, chopped
  • ½ tsp black mustard seeds
  • To assemble
  • 1 food-safe wick with metal tab
  • 1 straight-sided 8 oz pillar mold

Method

  1. 1

    Melt the tallow and butter over low heat. Whisk in the paprika, turmeric and salt until evenly golden. Do not let it smoke.

  2. 2

    Toast the yellow mustard seeds in a dry pan for 1 minute until they pop, then stir them into the fat.

  3. 3

    Fix the wick tab to the base of the mold with a drop of warm fat and hold the wick upright.

  4. 4

    Cool the mixture, stirring often, until it turns thick and opaque, so the seeds stay suspended. Pour into the mold.

  5. 5

    Chill for 2 hours, unmold and press the seed crown gently into the top. Trim the wick to ¼ in and light at the table.

Make ahead Wrapped tightly, the candle keeps for 2 weeks in the fridge. Bring it to room temperature for 30 minutes before lighting.
