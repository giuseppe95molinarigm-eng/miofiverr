# Smoked Paprika & Sea Salt Butter Candle
Sweet smoked paprika gives this butter its copper color and a woodsmoke aroma. A pinch of flaky sea salt adds crunch to each bite of grilled sourdough.

Makes 1 butter candle, about 8 servings · Prep 10 min · Chill 2 hr

## Ingredients
- ½ cup (113 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1½ tsp sweet smoked paprika
- ½ tsp finely grated lemon zest
- ¼ tsp fine sea salt
- 1 pinch flaky sea salt, to finish
- Grilled sourdough slices, for serving

You’ll also need a small cylindrical food-grade silicone mold (about ½ cup capacity) and a short piece of plain, undyed cotton kitchen twine for the decorative wick.

## Method
1. Season the butter. Mix the softened butter with the paprika, lemon zest, and fine sea salt until the color is uniform.
2. Fill the mold. Press the butter into the mold a spoonful at a time to minimize air pockets. Level the exposed surface and refrigerate for about 2 hours, until firm.
3. Add the wick. Unmold the butter onto a small ceramic plate. Make a shallow hole in the center of the top with a skewer and insert the twine so a short length stands upright. Sprinkle the flaky salt around it.
4. Serve. Let the butter soften for about 10 minutes. Keep the wick unlit and remove it before serving. Spread the butter on warm grilled sourdough.
