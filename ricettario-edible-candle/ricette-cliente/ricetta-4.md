# 04 · Maple & Cinnamon Butter Candle
Maple syrup and cinnamon make a soft, lightly sweet butter that melts into the pockets of a hot waffle.

Makes 1 butter candle, about 8 servings · Prep 10 min · Chill 2 hr

## Ingredients
- ½ cup (113 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 tbsp pure maple syrup
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 pinch fine sea salt
- Warm waffles and extra maple syrup, to serve

You’ll also need a small round food-grade silicone mold (about ⅔ cup capacity) and a short piece of plain, undyed cotton kitchen twine for the decorative wick.

## Method
1. Mix the butter. Stir the softened butter with the cinnamon and salt until evenly speckled. Add the maple syrup a teaspoon at a time, stirring well after each addition.
2. Shape and chill. Press the butter into the mold with the back of a spoon. Smooth the surface and refrigerate for about 2 hours, until firm enough to unmold.
3. Add the wick. Turn the butter out onto a small saucer. Make a shallow hole in the center with a skewer and insert the twine so a short length stands upright.
4. Serve. Let the butter soften for about 10 minutes. Keep the wick unlit and remove it before serving. Spoon the butter over hot waffles and finish with a drizzle of maple syrup.
