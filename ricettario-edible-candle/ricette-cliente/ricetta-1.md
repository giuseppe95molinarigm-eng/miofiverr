# Roasted Garlic & Rosemary Butter Candle
Sweet roasted garlic and fragrant rosemary turn a simple butter spread into a centerpiece for warm, crusty bread.

Makes 1 butter candle, about 8 servings · Prep 10 min · Roast 35–45 min · Chill 2 hr

## Ingredients
- 1 small head garlic
- 1 tsp olive oil
- ½ cup (113 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 tsp finely chopped fresh rosemary
- ¼ tsp fine sea salt
- ⅛ tsp freshly ground black pepper
- Toasted baguette slices, for serving

You’ll also need a small food-grade silicone spiral mold (about ½ cup capacity) and a short piece of plain, undyed cotton kitchen twine for the decorative wick.

## Method
1. Roast the garlic. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Slice the top off the garlic head to expose the cloves. Drizzle with olive oil, wrap in foil, and roast for 35–45 minutes, until soft and golden. Let cool.
2. Mix the butter. Squeeze 4 roasted cloves into a bowl and mash until smooth. Stir in the softened butter, rosemary, salt, and pepper.
3. Fill the mold. Press the butter into the mold with the back of a spoon to push it into the grooves. Smooth the exposed surface and refrigerate for about 2 hours, until firm.
4. Add the wick. Unmold onto a small ceramic plate. Use a skewer to make a shallow hole in the center of the top, then insert the cotton twine so a short length stands upright.
5. Serve. Let the butter soften for about 10 minutes. Keep the wick unlit and remove it before serving. Spread the butter over warm baguette slices.
