# 03 · Fig & Honey Butter Candle
Fig preserves leave little flecks of fruit in this honey-sweetened butter. Serve it with warm brioche, fresh figs, and toasted walnuts for crunch.

Makes 1 butter candle, about 8 servings · Prep 10 min · Chill 2 hr

## Ingredients
- ½ cup (113 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 tbsp thick fig preserves
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 pinch fine sea salt
- Toasted brioche, quartered fresh figs, and toasted walnuts, to serve

You’ll also need a small fluted food-grade silicone mold (about ⅔ cup capacity) and a short piece of plain, undyed cotton kitchen twine for the decorative wick.

## Method
1. Mix the butter. Stir the softened butter, honey, and salt until smooth. Fold in the fig preserves, leaving a few streaks and flecks of fruit.
2. Fill the mold. Press the butter into the fluted mold with the back of a spoon to fill the grooves. Smooth the exposed surface and refrigerate for about 2 hours, until firm.
3. Add the wick. Unmold onto a small ceramic plate. Make a shallow hole in the center of the top with a skewer and insert the twine so a short length stands upright.
4. Serve. Let the butter soften for about 10 minutes. Keep the wick unlit and remove it before serving. Spread the butter on warm brioche and serve with the fresh figs and toasted walnuts.
