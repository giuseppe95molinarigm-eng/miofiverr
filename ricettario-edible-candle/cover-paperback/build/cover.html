The Centerpiece You Can
Spread on Warm Bread

Shaped like elegant pillar candles but made entirely of flavored butter, edible candles are the easiest way to turn an ordinary meal into an occasion. In this beautifully photographed collection, food stylist Lauren Whitmore shows you how to make them with a simple silicone mold, a few pantry ingredients and ten minutes of hands-on time, from roasted garlic and smoked paprika to fig, honey and maple cinnamon.

Inside You’ll Find

  • Sweet & savory butter candles for every season
  • A simple four-step base method
  • Make-ahead, storage and serving notes
  • Pairings for boards, breads, brunch & holidays
  • Easy silicone-mold shaping, no special skills
  • Full-color photography throughout
LW

About the Author

Lauren Whitmore is a food stylist and recipe developer from Vermont, where autumn is the longest and most delicious season of the year. She has spent more than a decade styling harvest tables, brunches and holiday menus, and believes every gathering deserves one dish people talk about long after the plates are cleared.
